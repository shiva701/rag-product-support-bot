# Zentro A5 User Manual

## First-Time Setup
Press and hold the power button (right side) for 3 seconds to turn the phone on.
Choose your language, connect to Wi-Fi, and sign in with or create a RAG account.
Setup takes about 10 minutes. Charge the phone to at least 20% before starting.

## Inserting a SIM Card
Push the SIM ejector tool into the small hole on the SIM tray (bottom edge, left of the USB-C port).
Place a nano-SIM in the tray with the gold contacts facing down and slide the tray back in until it is flush.
A tray that is not fully closed reduces water resistance.

## Setting Up an eSIM
Go to Settings > Network > SIMs > Add eSIM, then scan the QR code from your carrier.
The phone must be connected to Wi-Fi to download the eSIM. You can store up to 5 eSIM profiles,
but only one eSIM can be active at a time alongside the physical SIM.

## Transferring Data from Your Old Phone
During setup, choose Copy from another phone. Connect both phones with the USB-C cable,
or use the Zentro Switch app over Wi-Fi. Zentro Switch works from Android 10 or later and iOS 15 or later.
Contacts, photos, messages, and most apps transfer. Banking and authenticator apps must be set up again.

## Charging the Phone
Use a USB-C Power Delivery charger. For the fastest charging, use a 45 W charger and the cable from the box.
The phone shows "Fast charging" on the lock screen when charging above 25 W.
For wireless charging, place the back of the phone on the center of a Qi-compatible charger; remove thick or metal cases first.

## Battery Care
Turn on Adaptive Charging in Settings > Battery to hold the charge at 80% overnight and finish just before your alarm.
You can also set a fixed charge limit of 80% or 90% in Settings > Battery > Charge limit.
Avoid leaving the phone in a hot car or in direct sunlight. The phone stops charging above 45°C (113°F) to protect the battery.

## Battery Saver
Turn on Battery Saver in Settings > Battery or from Quick Settings. It limits background activity,
lowers the refresh rate to 60 Hz, and turns off the always-on display.
Extreme Battery Saver keeps only the apps you pick and can extend the last 10% of battery by several hours.

## Fingerprint and Face Unlock
The fingerprint sensor is under the display, near the bottom of the screen.
Add up to 4 fingerprints in Settings > Security > Fingerprint. Face Unlock is set up in Settings > Security > Face Unlock.
Face Unlock is less secure than a fingerprint or PIN and cannot be used to approve payments.
After a restart, the phone always asks for your PIN, pattern, or password first.

## Camera Modes
Open the Camera app and swipe between modes: Photo, Portrait, Night, Video, Pro, and Macro.
Night mode turns on automatically in low light; hold the phone still for 2 to 4 seconds.
Tap 0.6x, 1x, or 3x to switch between the ultra-wide, main, and telephoto cameras.
Pro mode lets you set ISO, shutter speed, and white balance, and can save RAW files.

## Taking a Screenshot
Press the power button and the volume down button at the same time.
For a scrolling screenshot, tap Capture more in the preview that appears.
To record the screen, use Screen Record in Quick Settings.

## Software Updates
Go to Settings > System > Software update > Check for updates. Updates download over Wi-Fi by default.
The battery must be at least 30% charged, or the phone must be plugged in, to install an update.
Installation takes 10 to 20 minutes and the phone restarts once.

## Backing Up Your Phone
Go to Settings > System > Backup and turn on RAG Cloud Backup. The phone backs up automatically
once a day when it is charging, locked, and on Wi-Fi. Every RAG account includes 15 GB of free backup storage.
Photos and videos are backed up separately in the Gallery app under Settings > Cloud sync.

## Factory Reset
A factory reset erases everything on the phone. Back up first.
Go to Settings > System > Reset > Erase all data (factory reset) and enter your PIN.
After the reset, you must sign in with the RAG account that was last used on the phone (theft protection).
Remove your RAG account in Settings > Accounts before selling or giving away the phone.

## Restarting and Force Restart
To restart, hold the power button and tap Restart.
If the phone is frozen, force a restart by holding the power button and the volume down button together
for 10 seconds until the Zentro logo appears. A force restart does not erase any data.

## Safe Mode
Safe Mode starts the phone with only the built-in apps, which helps find an app that is causing problems.
Hold the power button, then touch and hold Power off until "Restart in Safe Mode" appears, and tap OK.
Restart the phone normally to leave Safe Mode.

## Using NFC and Contactless Payments
Turn on NFC in Settings > Connected devices > NFC. Add a card in the RAG Wallet app.
To pay, unlock the phone and hold its back, near the cameras, against the payment terminal.
A screen lock (PIN, pattern, password, or fingerprint) is required for payments.

## Do Not Disturb and Focus Modes
Swipe down twice and tap Do Not Disturb to silence calls and notifications.
In Settings > Focus you can create schedules, such as Sleep from 10 PM to 7 AM, and choose
which contacts and apps are still allowed to notify you. Alarms always sound.
