# Frequently Asked Questions

## Is the K2 BPA-free?
Yes. All parts that touch water are stainless steel or BPA-free plastic.

## Can I use the K2 outside North America?
No. The K2 is designed for 120 V power only. Using it with a voltage converter
voids the warranty.
Yes, you can use the K2 in North America, including the United States and Canada,
which use 120 V power. It cannot be used in regions with 220-240 V power, such as
Europe, the UK, India, and Australia.

## Does the K2 work with voice assistants?
Yes, once paired with the RAG Home app, you can link it to supported
voice assistants from the app's Integrations screen.

## Can I schedule the kettle to start at a set time?
Yes. In the RAG Home app, open the kettle, tap Schedule, and choose a time
and temperature. The kettle must be on its base with water in it.

## Where can I buy replacement parts?
Replacement lids and bases are sold at rag.example/parts.
