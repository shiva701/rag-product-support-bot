# Zentro A5 Frequently Asked Questions

## Does the Zentro A5 come with a charger?
No. The box includes a USB-C to USB-C cable but no charger. Any USB-C Power Delivery charger works;
the 45 W RAG Mobile charger gives the fastest charging and is sold separately at rag.example/accessories.

## Can I use two SIMs at the same time?
Yes. The Zentro A5 supports dual SIM with one physical nano-SIM and one eSIM. Both lines can receive
calls and texts. You choose which line is used for mobile data in Settings > Network > SIMs.
Two physical SIM cards cannot be used; there is only one SIM slot.

## Does the Zentro A5 have a headphone jack?
No. Use Bluetooth headphones or USB-C headphones. A USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter is sold separately.

## Can I add a microSD card?
No. The Zentro A5 has no microSD card slot. Choose the 128 GB or 256 GB model, and use
RAG Cloud Backup or a USB-C flash drive for extra space.

## Is the Zentro A5 unlocked?
Phones bought from rag.example are carrier-unlocked and work with any carrier that supports the phone's
4G and 5G bands. Phones bought from a carrier may be locked to that carrier; ask the carrier to unlock them.

## Does the Zentro A5 work internationally?
Yes. The charger input range is 100-240 V, and an unlocked Zentro A5 supports 4G and sub-6 GHz 5G bands
used in North America, Europe, and most of Asia. It does not support mmWave 5G.
You can add a local eSIM plan while traveling.

## Can I swim or shower with the phone?
No. The IP68 rating covers accidental contact with fresh water, such as rain or a brief drop in a sink.
Do not swim, shower, or use a sauna with the phone. If it gets wet, rinse off anything other than fresh water,
dry it with a soft cloth, and do not charge it with a cable until the port is dry.

## How long will the Zentro A5 get software updates?
The Zentro A5 gets 5 years of operating system upgrades and 6 years of security updates from its launch date.

## Does it support wireless charging?
Yes. The Zentro A5 charges wirelessly at up to 15 W on Qi-compatible chargers and can charge
earbuds or a watch from its back at up to 5 W (turn on Battery Share in Quick Settings).

## Can I replace the battery myself?
No. The battery is not user-replaceable, and opening the phone voids the warranty and the water resistance.
RAG Mobile service centers replace batteries; see the warranty document for coverage and pricing.

## Where can I buy cases and screen protectors?
Official cases, screen protectors, chargers, and adapters are sold at rag.example/accessories.
Use screen protectors made for the Zentro A5 so the fingerprint sensor keeps working.

## How do I find a lost phone?
Sign in at rag.example/find with your RAG account to see the phone on a map, make it ring,
lock it with a message, or erase it. Find My Phone must be on in Settings > Security
(it is on by default), and the phone must be powered on and connected to the internet.

## How do I check my phone's IMEI and serial number?
Dial *#06# in the Phone app, or go to Settings > About phone. The IMEI is also printed on the SIM tray and on the box.
