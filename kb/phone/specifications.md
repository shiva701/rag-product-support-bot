# Zentro A5 Specifications

## Display
6.4-inch AMOLED display, 2400 x 1080 resolution (Full HD+), 120 Hz adaptive refresh rate.
Peak brightness is 1,800 nits. The screen is protected by ArmorGlass 3.
An always-on display option shows the clock and notifications while the phone is locked.

## Processor and Memory
The Zentro A5 uses the Helix H3 octa-core processor with 8 GB of RAM.
Storage options are 128 GB or 256 GB. Storage cannot be expanded; there is no microSD card slot.

## Battery and Charging Specifications
Battery capacity is 4,800 mAh. Wired fast charging is up to 45 W over USB-C (USB Power Delivery).
Wireless charging is up to 15 W on Qi-compatible chargers. Reverse wireless charging is up to 5 W.
With the 45 W charger, the battery charges from 0 to 50% in about 25 minutes and to 100% in about 70 minutes.
Typical battery life is a full day of mixed use, or up to 22 hours of video playback.

## Rear Cameras
Three rear cameras:
- Main: 50 MP, f/1.8, optical image stabilization (OIS).
- Ultra-wide: 12 MP, f/2.2, 120-degree field of view.
- Telephoto: 10 MP, f/2.4, 3x optical zoom, OIS.
Digital zoom goes up to 30x. Rear video recording is up to 4K at 60 frames per second.

## Front Camera
12 MP front camera, f/2.2, with autofocus. Front video recording is up to 4K at 30 frames per second.

## Connectivity
5G (sub-6 GHz), 4G LTE, Wi-Fi 6E, Bluetooth 5.3, NFC for contactless payments, and GPS.
The phone has one physical nano-SIM slot and supports one eSIM, so two lines can be active at once.
The USB-C port supports USB 3.2 data transfer and video output. There is no 3.5 mm headphone jack.

## Dimensions and Weight
152.4 x 71.2 x 7.9 mm. Weight: 178 g. The frame is recycled aluminum with a matte glass back.

## Colors
The Zentro A5 comes in three colors: Graphite, Sage Green, and Pearl White.
Pearl White is available only in the 256 GB model.

## Water and Dust Resistance
The Zentro A5 is rated IP68: dust-tight and tested in fresh water up to 1.5 meters deep for 30 minutes.
The rating does not cover salt water, pool water, soapy water, or hot water (showers, saunas).
Water resistance can decrease over time with normal wear. Liquid damage is not covered by the warranty.

## Operating System and Updates
The Zentro A5 ships with ZentroOS 4, based on Android 16.
RAG Mobile provides 5 years of operating system upgrades and 6 years of security updates
from the phone's launch date. Security updates arrive monthly.

## What Is in the Box
The Zentro A5, a 1-meter USB-C to USB-C cable, a SIM ejector tool, and a quick start guide.
A charger is not included; the 45 W RAG Mobile charger is sold separately.
A case and earphones are not included.

## Price

Prices at carriers and other retailers may differ.
