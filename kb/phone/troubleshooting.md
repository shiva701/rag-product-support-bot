# Zentro A5 Troubleshooting

## Phone Will Not Turn On
Charge the phone for at least 15 minutes with a charger and cable you know work.
A fully drained battery can take up to 5 minutes before the charging icon appears.
Then force a restart: hold the power button and volume down together for 10 seconds.
If there is still no response after 30 minutes of charging, contact RAG Mobile support.

## Battery Drains Quickly
Go to Settings > Battery > Usage to see which apps use the most power, and restrict background use for them.
Lower the screen brightness, turn off the always-on display, and turn on Battery Saver.
Battery drain is normal for the first 2 to 3 days after setup or a major update while the phone finishes indexing.
If Settings > Battery > Battery health shows capacity below 80%, the battery may need service.

## Phone Is Overheating
The phone can get warm while fast charging, gaming, recording 4K video, or using it as a hotspot.
If a temperature warning appears, stop charging, close demanding apps, remove the case, and move
the phone out of direct sunlight. Do not put it in a refrigerator or freezer.
The phone dims the screen, slows charging, and may turn off the camera flash until it cools down.

## Phone Charges Slowly or Not at All
Check for "Fast charging" on the lock screen; without it, the charger or cable does not support 25 W or more.
Try a different USB-C Power Delivery charger and cable. Clean lint out of the USB-C port with a
wooden or plastic toothpick; never use metal. Charging slows above 80% and when the phone is hot; both are normal.
If a charge limit is set in Settings > Battery, the phone stops at 80% or 90% by design.

## Moisture Detected in the USB-C Port
If a water drop icon or "Moisture detected" message appears, unplug the cable right away.
Wired charging is disabled until the port is dry, which usually takes 1 to 2 hours.
Leave the phone in a dry, ventilated place with the port facing down. Do not use a hair dryer or put the phone in rice.
You can use wireless charging while you wait.

## No Service or Weak Mobile Signal
Turn Airplane mode on for 10 seconds, then off. Restart the phone.
Remove and reinsert the SIM card, and check that the SIM is turned on in Settings > Network > SIMs.
If the problem continues, go to Settings > System > Reset > Reset network settings.
This clears saved Wi-Fi networks and Bluetooth pairings but no personal data.

## Wi-Fi Will Not Connect or Keeps Dropping
Forget the network in Settings > Network > Wi-Fi and join it again with the password.
Restart your router and the phone. Keep the phone within range of the router.
If only one network fails, the problem is likely the router. If every network fails, reset
network settings in Settings > System > Reset.

## Bluetooth Device Will Not Pair
Put the accessory in pairing mode and keep it within 1 meter of the phone.
In Settings > Connected devices, forget the device if it is listed, then pair again.
Turn Bluetooth off and on. The phone can stay connected to up to 5 Bluetooth devices at once.

## Touchscreen Is Unresponsive
Clean and dry the screen and your hands. Remove any screen protector that is not made for the Zentro A5.
Force a restart by holding the power button and volume down for 10 seconds.
If touch works in Safe Mode, an app is the cause; uninstall recently installed apps.
Turn on Settings > Display > Touch sensitivity when you use a screen protector.

## Photos Are Blurry
Wipe the camera lenses with a soft, dry cloth. Tap the subject on the screen to focus.
Hold the phone steady in low light, or use Night mode. For close-ups nearer than 10 cm, switch to Macro mode.
If the camera cannot focus at any distance, or you hear rattling from the camera, contact RAG Mobile support.

## Storage Is Almost Full
Go to Settings > Storage > Free up space to remove duplicate photos, large files, and unused apps.
Turn on Cloud sync in the Gallery app, then use Free up device storage to delete photos that are already backed up.
Keep at least 10% of storage free; the phone slows down and cannot install updates when storage is nearly full.

## An App Keeps Crashing
Update the app from the app store, then restart the phone.
If it still crashes, go to Settings > Apps, choose the app, and tap Storage > Clear cache.
As a last step, tap Clear storage (this signs you out and removes the app's data) or reinstall the app.

## Phone Is Slow or Freezing
Restart the phone; restarting once a week is recommended. Install any pending software update.
Free up storage if less than 10% is available. Check whether the problem goes away in Safe Mode.
If the phone is still slow after these steps, back up your data and do a factory reset.

## Fingerprint Sensor Does Not Recognize Me
Clean the screen where the sensor is and make sure your finger is dry.
Delete the fingerprint and add it again in Settings > Security > Fingerprint, covering the edges of your finger.
Screen protectors thicker than 0.3 mm can block the sensor. After 5 failed attempts, the phone asks for your PIN.

## No Sound During Calls
Raise the volume with the side buttons during a call. Check that the phone is not connected to a Bluetooth device.
Remove any case or screen protector that covers the earpiece at the top of the screen.
Test with speakerphone; if the speaker works but the earpiece does not, contact RAG Mobile support.

## Forgot PIN, Pattern, or Password
For security, RAG Mobile cannot unlock the phone or recover a forgotten PIN.
Sign in at rag.example/find with your RAG account and choose Erase device, then set the phone up again
and restore from your backup. Data that was not backed up is lost.
