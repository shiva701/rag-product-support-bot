# Warranty and Returns

## Warranty Coverage
The Kettlewise K2 has a 2-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.
It covers defects in materials and workmanship, including heating element and
sensor failures.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover
Damage from misuse, including heating liquids other than water, immersing the base,
using abrasive cleaners, or operating on non-120 V power. Cosmetic wear and
scratches. Damage from failure to descale as recommended.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim
Email support@rag.example with your order number, the kettle's serial number
(on the bottom of the base), and a short description of the problem.
RAG replies within 2 business days.

## Returns
Unused kettles in original packaging can be returned within 30 days of delivery
for a full refund. Used kettles can be returned within 30 days for store credit only.
Return shipping is free for defective items.
