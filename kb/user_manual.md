# Kettlewise K2 User Manual

## Specifications
Capacity: 1.5 liters (minimum fill 0.5 liters). Power: 1500 W, 120 V.
Temperature presets: 70°C, 80°C, 90°C, and 100°C (boil).
Keep-warm mode holds the selected temperature for up to 60 minutes.

## First Use
Before first use, fill the kettle to the MAX line, boil, and discard the water.
Repeat twice to remove manufacturing residue.

## Setting the Temperature
Press the TEMP button to cycle through presets. The selected preset lights up.
Press START to begin heating. The kettle beeps three times when it reaches temperature.

## Keep-Warm Mode
Hold the TEMP button for 2 seconds after heating to turn on keep-warm.
The KEEP WARM light turns on. Keep-warm turns off automatically after 60 minutes
or when the kettle is lifted from the base for more than 2 minutes.

## App Pairing
Download the RAG Home app. Hold START and TEMP together for 5 seconds
until the light blinks blue. In the app, tap Add Device and choose Kettlewise K2.
The kettle supports 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi only; 5 GHz networks are not supported.

## Descaling
Descale every 4 weeks in hard-water areas, every 8 weeks otherwise.
Mix 1 part white vinegar with 2 parts water to the MAX line. Bring to boil,
let stand for 30 minutes, then empty and rinse three times with fresh water.
Do not use abrasive pads inside the kettle.

## Cleaning
Wipe the outside with a damp cloth. The kettle and base are not dishwasher safe.
Never immerse the base in water.

## Safety
Use the kettle for heating water only. Do not heat milk, soup, or other liquids.
Do not operate the kettle when empty; the boil-dry protection will shut it off,
but repeated dry operation can damage the heating element.
