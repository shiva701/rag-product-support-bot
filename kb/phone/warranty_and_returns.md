# Zentro A5 Warranty and Returns

## Warranty Coverage
The Zentro A5 has a 1-year limited warranty from the date of purchase.
It covers defects in materials and workmanship, including the display, buttons, cameras, speakers, and charging port.
The cable included in the box is covered for 6 months.

## Battery Warranty
The battery is covered for 1 year. RAG Mobile replaces it free of charge if battery health
(Settings > Battery > Battery health) falls below 80% during the warranty period.
Normal capacity loss above 80% is not a defect.

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover
Accidental damage such as cracked screens and dents. Liquid damage, even though the phone is IP68 rated.
Damage from unauthorized repairs or from opening the phone. Problems caused by modified or unofficial software.
Cosmetic wear such as scratches. Lost or stolen phones.

## How to Make a Warranty Claim
Start a claim at rag.example/support or email mobile-support@rag.example with your IMEI,
proof of purchase, and a description of the problem. RAG Mobile replies within 2 business days.
Back up and factory reset the phone, and remove your RAG account, before sending it in.
Repairs usually take 5 to 7 business days after the phone arrives.

## Out-of-Warranty Repairs
RAG Mobile service centers repair phones that are out of warranty or have accidental damage.
Screen replacement costs $149. Battery replacement costs $69. Back glass replacement costs $79.
Repairs come with a 90-day warranty on the replaced part.

## RAG Care+ Protection Plan
RAG Care+ extends the warranty to 2 years and adds accidental damage cover for drops and liquid damage.
It costs $7 per month or $129 for 2 years, and must be bought within 60 days of purchasing the phone.
Each accidental damage claim has a $49 service fee, with up to 2 claims per year.

## Returns
A Zentro A5 bought from rag.example can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund
if it is in like-new condition with all original contents. A 15% restocking fee applies if the phone
is damaged or missing accessories. Return shipping is free. Refunds are issued within 5 business days
of receiving the phone. Phones bought from a carrier or retailer follow that seller's return policy.

## Trade-In
RAG Mobile accepts trade-ins of older phones toward a Zentro A5. Get an estimate at rag.example/trade-in;
the final value is confirmed after inspection and is paid as an instant discount or as store credit.
